The Product & Tech

60% pre-cured real liquid gel strips that harden in natural daylight (HeyHae-style solar gel tech). No UV lamp required.

Target Customer

Women (and men) tired of $80+, 2-hour salon visits who want salon-grade maintenance as an easy at-home self-care ritual.

Packaging Rules

HeyHae box proportions. Fully enclosed (no plastic window, protects from UV) & no AI hands—just a 5-nail 3D gloss lineup on the front.

Assortment Focus

North American wearable staples (neutrals, reds/maroons, cat-eye, ring-finger accents) + Holiday/Winter LTO drops.
