Brand Identity & Pouch Packaging Review

Sundae Reset vs. Sun Kissed

Compare the physical flat-pouch packaging contenders, typography systems, and color palettes across both brand directions. Click any image to inspect full screen.

The Product & Tech

60% pre-cured real liquid gel strips that harden in natural daylight (HeyHae-style solar gel tech). NO LED LAMP NEEDED.

Target Customer

Women (and men) tired of $80+, 2-hour salon visits who want salon-grade maintenance as an easy at-home self-care ritual.

Pouch Packaging Format

Tall, slim, rounded-corner flat beauty pouch (UV-protective, no plastic window) with a whimsical 5-nail cascade printed on the front.

Assortment Focus

North American wearable staples (Crème Brûlée neutral, sheer nudes, classic reds, deep maroons) + Holiday/Winter LTO drops.

Concept 01 (C1) • Bold & Whimsical

Sundae Reset

Sweet, indulgent, treat-yourself, dreamy, and bold. Ties together Sun (how it cures), Sundae (delicious flavours), and the weekly Sunday Reset self-care ritual.

Concept 02 (C2) • Warm Clean-Girl Luxury

Sun Kissed

Warm, radiant, effortless, and high-end. Inspired by Summer Fridays, Rhode, Loewe, and Crown Affair—turning natural daylight and shadow into an iconic beauty packaging signature.

Pouch Packaging Options (Shade 01: Cashmere)
